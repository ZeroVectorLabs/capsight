# Code of Conduct

Deepacket is a tool for defenders, students and researchers. Keep this project a place where anyone can ask a question or send a fix.

## Expected

- Be respectful, including when you disagree.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you assume.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Help newcomers. Everyone started somewhere.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission.
- Posting real captures that contain other people's credentials or personal data.
- Using the project's spaces to plan attacks on systems you are not authorised to test.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainer through the contact on the [ZeroVectorLabs](https://github.com/ZeroVectorLabs) profile. Reports are handled in confidence. The maintainer may edit or remove content and block people who break this code.
